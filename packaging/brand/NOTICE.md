# Synchro brand assets

`synchro-mark.svg` is the Synchro application mark.

`synchro-wordmark.svg` is outlined artwork generated from the **Outrun Future**
typeface by PressGang Studios / Andeh Pinkard (2016). The font binary is not
distributed with Synchro and the application does not require it at runtime.

The outlined glyph design is excluded from the repository's general software
license grant. It is included for non-commercial Synchro distribution under the
typeface terms supplied with the original font. Verify those original terms
before commercial redistribution or reuse of the wordmark outside Synchro.
